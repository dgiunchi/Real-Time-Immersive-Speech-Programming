Participant Information Sheet

Study title

Understanding User Attribution and Recovery from Interaction Failures in LLM-Based VR Authoring Systems.

Researchers

Akbar Juraev (axj509@student.bham.ac.uk)  |  Amal (axa2610@student.bham.ac.uk)

What is this study about?

This study looks at how people work with a system that builds things in virtual reality (VR) from spoken instructions. You will describe what you want using your voice and watch it appear in the scene. We are studying the system’s behaviour, not your ability — there is no skill being tested.

What will I be asked to do?

You will complete a short background questionnaire, then 6 short voice-controlled tasks in a VR scene, each followed by a couple of quick questions. Afterwards you will complete some short questionnaires and take part in a short interview (5–10 minutes). The whole visit takes about 45 minutes, in one session — no follow-up required.

Do I have to take part?

No — participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any point during the session, without giving a reason and without any penalty.

Will I be compensated?

Yes — you will receive a £5 Amazon voucher or a bank transfer as a thank-you for your time, regardless of how the session goes.

Who can take part?

  • 18 years or older
  • Comfortable speaking English out loud
  • Normal or corrected-to-normal vision
  • Do not usually experience significant motion sickness

What is recorded?

Three things. What happens in the scene — what you asked for, what appeared, how long each task took. Where your head was pointing, so we know whether you were looking at the feedback when it appeared. And, if you agree to it below, a sound recording of your voice each time you hold the trigger and speak to the system.

The sound recordings are what let us measure how people speak to a system that has just failed them — whether they get louder, or slower, or repeat themselves. Agreeing to them is optional, and you can take part fully without: if you decline, no sound is saved at any point.

Will my data be kept confidential?

Yes. All data will be anonymised using a participant ID instead of your name. Nothing recorded is labelled with your name, including the sound files. Data will be stored securely on University-approved storage and only the research team will have access.

Are there any risks?

No significant risks are anticipated. As with any VR use, if you feel any discomfort you may stop at any time. Note: some parts of the tasks are designed to produce an unexpected or ‘wrong’ outcome on purpose, as part of the research design — this is expected and is not a sign that you have done anything wrong. You will be told more about this at the end of the session (see Debrief, below).

What happens at the end?

After the interview you will be fully debriefed: we will explain exactly how the study worked, including that the unexpected outcomes were pre-planned by the research team and were never caused by anything you did. You are welcome to ask questions at this point.

Who can I contact with questions?

Either researcher: Akbar Juraev (axj509@student.bham.ac.uk) or Amal (axa2610@student.bham.ac.uk), or the project supervisor.
