Build a scene by talking to it

A study on how people work with a system that makes what they describe. One visit, about 45 minutes. No experience needed.

Before you sign up

Please read the participant information sheet:

Participant information sheet ↗

It explains what the session involves, what happens to your data, and your right to stop at any time. Please read it before booking — signing up is not consent, and you will be asked to consent again in person.

What happens on the day

  • A few background questions (about 5 minutes)
  • Six short building tasks in VR, using your voice
  • A questionnaire and a short chat about how it went
  • That’s everything — there is no follow-up and nothing to do afterwards

Pick a slot

Your name and email are stored only to arrange the session and are kept separately from the study data. They are deleted once the study is finished.
